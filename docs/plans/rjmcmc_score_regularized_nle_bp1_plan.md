# BP1 plan: score-regularized projected marginal NLE

## Status and motivation

This is a new, observation-blind experiment after the calibrated finite
projected-bank likelihood stopped at G4.  The source simulator itself passed:
it reproduced the frozen projected Gamma--Dirichlet catalogue with bounded
memory.  The raw equal-weight bank did not converge as a density estimator at
\(S=65536\) for any \(q\in\{16,32,64,128\}\).

Two earlier normalized neural likelihoods also stopped:

- a conditional FlowJAX triangular spline flow; and
- an independent `sbi`/`nflows` neural spline flow.

Both learned the complete noisy residual using likelihood training alone.
Both produced finite, normalized, replayable artifacts, but zero of 24
development artifacts passed the retained-mass gradient gate.  This plan does
not repeat either architecture with more layers, epochs, or simulations.

The new feature is exact simulator-derived score supervision.  It trains a
normalized conditional coupling flow on the noisy projected marginal while
directly supervising its retained-root-mass score, the scientific derivative
that every previous NLE missed.  The exact observation-score identity remains
a validation diagnostic rather than a training term, avoiding a
\(q\)-dimensional second-derivative objective at PARIS rank.

The choice to learn the noisy leading coordinates is deliberate.  A generic
flow for the bounded noise-free pushforward has no analytic
flow--Gaussian-convolution density.  Evaluating that convolution with an
uncontrolled Monte Carlo sum would recreate the source-bank failure.  Here
the fixed Gaussian convolution is included in the training simulator, while
its exact score identity supplies the physics-informed constraint.

The completed source-bank result is
[`rjmcmc_chunked_projected_bank_g4_bp1_report.md`](rjmcmc_chunked_projected_bank_g4_bp1_report.md).
The mathematical background and earlier learned-model evidence are in the
sibling `inversions-knowledge` files:

- `docs/research-questions/learning-non-gaussian-marginal-models.md`; and
- `docs/derivations/non-gaussian-aggregation-error-by-marginalization.md`.

## Scope

The experiment remains root-only and fixed-context.  It approximates the
conditional marginal of one common native Gamma--Dirichlet model after one
fixed observation-space projection.  It does not authorize:

- RJ acceptance or inference on \(K\);
- data-dependent basis or partition weights;
- use of approximate evidence differences as structural information;
- access to the protected catalogue;
- a real PARIS posterior; or
- output under `PARIS_inversions`.

Any exact representation-only evidence is invariant to partition, \(K\),
computational chunk, sample seed, training layout, and network architecture.
Finite differences across any of those choices are approximation leakage.

## Probability target

Let \(\xi_q\in\mathbb R^q\) be the leading unit-root projected allocation
residual generated by the exact native simulator, with

\[
\mathbb E(\xi_q)=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{Cov}(\xi_q)
=
\operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_q).
\]

After measurement-noise whitening, the leading projected observation
residual is

\[
Y_q=T\xi_q+\epsilon_q,
\qquad
\epsilon_q\sim\mathcal N(0,I_q).
\]

For

\[
s_j(T)=\sqrt{1+T^2\lambda_j},
\qquad
S(T)=\operatorname{diag}\{s_j(T)\},
\]

the learner models the standardized variable

\[
X_q=S(T)^{-1}Y_q
\]

with a normalized conditional density

\[
q_\phi(x\mid\tau),
\qquad
\tau=\log T.
\]

The scientific Gamma prior has \(T>0\).  The evaluator nevertheless has a
literal \(T=0\) branch that returns standard Gaussian measurement noise
without evaluating \(\log T\), so the vanishing-aggregation-error limit is
exact.

The resulting leading-coordinate likelihood is

\[
\log \widehat p_\phi(y_q\mid T)
=
\log q_\phi\{S(T)^{-1}y_q\mid\log T\}
-\sum_{j=1}^q\log s_j(T).
\]

The scientific retained-mass derivative holds \(y_q\), not \(x\), fixed.
Therefore the evaluator must use the complete chain rule

\[
\boxed{
\partial_\tau\log\widehat p_\phi(y_q\mid T)
=
\partial_\tau\log q_\phi(x\mid\tau)
-\{\nabla_x\log q_\phi(x\mid\tau)\}^{\mathsf T}
S^{-1}\dot Sx
-\operatorname{tr}(S^{-1}\dot S).
}
\]

Training supervises the partial derivative in standardized coordinates;
scientific gradient gates exercise this boxed fixed-\(y_q\) derivative.

Directions \(q+1{:}r\) retain the existing analytic Gaussian moment closure
with variance \(1+T^2\lambda_j\).  Directions beyond the authenticated
aggregation-residual spectrum contain measurement noise only.  The
observation-scale Jacobian and fixed offset translation remain explicit.

This is a normalized hybrid likelihood.  It is not an exact continuous
Dirichlet marginal when \(q<r\), and the Gaussian complement must be audited
as approximation leakage.

## Exact simulator score identities

For one simulator draw

\[
y=T\xi+\epsilon,
\qquad
x=S(T)^{-1}y,
\]

the conditional Gaussian component log density in \(x\) is

\[
\ell_c(x,T,\xi)
=
-\frac12\lVert S(T)x-T\xi\rVert^2
+\log\det S(T)
-\frac q2\log(2\pi).
\]

Its observation score at the simulated pair is

\[
\boxed{
\nabla_x\ell_c=-S(T)\epsilon.
}
\]

Writing a dot for differentiation with respect to
\(\tau=\log T\),

\[
\dot s_j(T)=\frac{T^2\lambda_j}{s_j(T)},
\]

the simulated component mass score is

\[
\boxed{
\partial_\tau\ell_c
=
\epsilon^\mathsf T\{T\xi-\dot S(T)x\}
+\sum_j\frac{\dot s_j(T)}{s_j(T)}.
}
\]

Conditional expectation of either component score given \((x,T)\) is the
corresponding score of the exact marginal density.  Squared-error regression
against these targets is therefore Fisher-consistent.  It does not require a
pointwise PARIS likelihood oracle.

## Frozen learned architecture

Use an opt-in `nle` Pixi feature with:

- JAX/JAXlib in float64 CPU mode;
- FlowJAX `17.2.1`;
- Equinox, Optax, and Paramax versions resolved by that pinned FlowJAX
  environment; and
- no PyTorch, `sbi`, `nflows`, PyMC bridge, or protected-data dependency.

For \(q\ge2\), the conditional density is one FlowJAX coupling flow with:

```text
base distribution:       q-dimensional standard normal
transformer:              rational-quadratic spline
coupling layers:          8
spline knots:             8
spline interval:          [-5, 5]
conditioner:              standardized log T
conditioner hidden width: 128
conditioner depth:        2
activation:               tanh
density-prioritized map:  invert=true
parameter dtype:          float64
```

The \(q=1\) tiny-oracle specialization uses FlowJAX's conditional
one-dimensional masked-autoregressive rational-quadratic-spline flow with
the same knot interval, conditioner-network width/depth/activation, layer
count, inversion control, and initialization rule; a coupling split is
undefined in one dimension.

Coupling masks and permutations are deterministic functions of the full
initialization seed.  Two initialization seeds are fitted per task; the
independent model-selection domain chooses the lower composite validation
loss.  Architecture, rank, sample size, or objective weights are never chosen
from realized `mf`, evidence, or posterior results.

Condition standardization is analytic and cannot be fitted from a simulation
catalogue.  For a task whose native root total is
\(T\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(a_T,b_T)\), use

\[
c_T=\mathbb E(\log T)=\psi(a_T)-\log b_T,
\qquad
d_T=\{\operatorname{Var}(\log T)\}^{1/2}
=\{\psi_1(a_T)\}^{1/2},
\]

and pass \((\log T-c_T)/d_T\) to the conditioner.  Tiny cases use
\(a_T=\sum_i\alpha_i\) and their frozen common native rate \(b_T\).
The calibrated PARIS root uses \(a_T=b_T=43.742615510366136\), giving

```text
condition center c_T = -0.011474050604083352
condition scale  d_T =  0.15206677909671867
```

This rule is shared by every sample size, initialization, and source seed;
nested-\(S\) artifacts may not standardize the condition from their realized
training samples.

## Frozen training objective

For a batch of \((x,\tau,\xi,\epsilon)\), minimize

\[
\mathcal L
=
\frac1q\mathbb E[-\log q_\phi(x\mid\tau)]
+\mathbb E\left[
\left\{
\frac{
\partial_\tau\log q_\phi(x\mid\tau)
-g_\tau(x,T,\xi,\epsilon)}
{\sqrt q}
\right\}^2
\right],
\]

where \(g_\tau\) is the boxed mass-score target above.  Thus the NLL and
mass-score terms have fixed weights \(1,1\).  The observation-score error

\[
\frac1q\sum_j
\left\{
\frac{\partial_{x_j}\log q_\phi(x\mid\tau)}{s_j(T)}
+\epsilon_j
\right\}^2
\]

is evaluated on held-out domains but does not enter optimization or model
selection.

The same composite loss is used for early stopping and initialization
selection.  NLL, mass-score risk, and the held-out observation-score
diagnostic are reported separately.  Optimizer controls are:

```text
optimizer:                    Adam
learning rate:                5e-4
batch size:                   1024
score microbatch:              64
maximum epochs:               100
early-stopping patience:      10
internal validation fraction: 0.10
return best epoch:            true
```

Each optimizer batch is split into 16 score microbatches.  Parameter
gradients, including the mixed second derivatives created by the mass-score
term, are accumulated and averaged before one Adam update.  This
computational-memory control is part of the frozen training algorithm because
floating-point accumulation order can change a fitted artifact.

No loss weight, optimizer control, architecture control, or training support
may change after a development result is inspected.

## Frozen projection ranks

The authenticated analytic spectrum has numerical rank \(r=1381\).
Diagnostic trace retention is:

```text
q=128: 98.0593%
q=256: 99.6072%
q=512: 99.9640%
```

The earlier \(q=128\) direct bank was not a stable density reference.  This
plan therefore freezes \(q=256\) and \(q=512\) as a two-rank ladder.  Both
must pass.  Their complete hybrid likelihoods must agree under the frozen
leakage limits.  If they do, the smaller \(q=256\) is selected.  If they do
not, there is no rank lock; explained variance alone cannot overrule the
failure.

PARIS has no exact continuous likelihood oracle.  Agreement between these two
ranks is only an observation-blind stability/leakage test; it is not proof
that the \(q>512\) Gaussian complement is exact.

No \(q>512\) escalation is permitted inside this plan.

## Independent simulation domains

Every domain is generated without realized observations.

### Tiny exact-oracle programme

Reuse the frozen six root cases:

- near-Gaussian, skewed, and boundary-heavy allocation regimes; and
- two-cell and four-cell native allocations.

Use training sizes

\[
4096,\ 16384,\ 65536,\ 262144.
\]

Each task has separate training, internal-validation,
model-selection-validation, and development-test domains.  The two
initializations share data within a task but not initialization or optimizer
keys.

Within each tiny task, root masses are a scrambled one-dimensional Sobol
catalogue transformed through the exact
\(\operatorname{Gamma}(\sum_i\alpha_i,b_T)\) inverse CDF.  Allocation and
measurement-noise catalogues use separate domain-keyed scrambled Sobol
streams.  Larger training sizes are exact prefixes of the same task/source
catalogue.

Freeze the remaining tiny-domain controls as:

```text
development base seed:                 731
confirmation base seeds:               1877, 4099, 8317
model-selection validation draws:      65536
development reporting-test draws:      131072
public domains:                        training
                                       model-selection-validation
                                       development-reporting-test

                                       flow-initialization-1

                                       optimizer-1
allocation stream:                     balanced-dirichlet-allocation
root-mass stream:                      gamma-root-total
```

Every stream seed is the little-endian unsigned integer encoded by the first

```text

  "score-regularized-projected-root-nle-v1"
  || uint64_le(base_seed)
  || ascii(case_id)
  || ascii(public_domain)
  || ascii(stream_name)
```

Unknown or protected domain names are rejected before deriving a seed.  The
two initializations share the task's simulated arrays but have distinct
initialization and optimizer streams; these four private task streams use
`training` as the `public_domain` byte string in the formula above.
Independent model-selection loss chooses the lower composite loss, with
initialization index as the exact-tie breaker.

### PARIS programme

The training mass catalogue is a scrambled one-dimensional Sobol draw from
the calibrated \(\operatorname{Gamma}(a,a)\) prior, clipped only at the
binary64 inverse-CDF endpoints.  Allocation sources use the existing
stable-ID balanced-Dirichlet chunked simulator.  Gaussian noise uses a
separate scrambled Sobol catalogue before inverse-normal transformation.

Freeze:

```text
development source seed:          731
confirmation source seeds:        1877, 4099, 8317
training sample sizes:             65536, 262144
model-selection validation draws: 65536
development test draws:           131072
rank ladder:                       256, 512
```

The PARIS \(q=512,S=262144\) source is constructed once per source seed.
Every smaller \(S\) and \(q\) input is an exact row/coordinate prefix.  Sample
chunk \(C\) and projection microbatch \(P\) remain computational controls and
must not change projected results.

The 256-state observation-blind grid from the calibrated G4 experiment may be
used only as a reporting grid.  Training and model selection use fresh,
domain-keyed simulation catalogues.

## Gate sequence

### N0: implementation and identity

Before scientific fits:

1. derive and unit-test both simulator score identities;
2. test custom-loss gradients against direct JAX differentiation;
3. require normalized one- and two-dimensional densities by numerical
   integration;
4. recover the exact Gaussian, rank-zero, zero-mass, one-cell, and
   identical-footprint limits;
5. require sample/log-density consistency;
6. require exact observation-offset translation;
7. require cell, observation, and eigenvector-sign permutation invariance;
8. require strict float64 artifact serialization, separate-process replay,
   and malformed-context rejection;
9. run focused experimental tests, Ruff, and focused Pyright; and
10. publish the completion marker last.

### N1: six-case exact-oracle development

Run the 24 case/size tasks as four homogeneous six-case Slurm arrays, one
array for each frozen training size.  A task fits both frozen initializations
and publishes only after independent selection.  Submit the \(S=4096\) tier
first with a two-hour shared-node limit.  Before submitting each larger tier,
use completed smaller-tier Slurm `Elapsed` evidence to set a size-appropriate
walltime; walltime is a computational control only and cannot alter the
frozen scientific matrix, seeds, model, selection rule, or gate.  No N1 tier
may inherit the former 72-hour safety ceiling.

Reuse the exact tiny thresholds:

| Metric | Threshold |
|---|---:|
| Prior-weighted median absolute log-likelihood error | 0.05 nat |
| Posterior-weighted p99 absolute log-likelihood error | 0.20 nat |
| Scaled retained-mass gradient error | 0.05 |
| Absolute log-evidence error | 0.05 nat |
| Posterior-mean error | 0.05 reference SD |
| Posterior-SD relative error | 0.02 |
| Interval-endpoint error | 0.05 reference SD |

Also require finite normalized density, finite score risks, and independent
model-selection-versus-test NLL agreement no larger than
`max(0.02*q nat, 5*pooled NLL MCSE)`.  A common lock requires an
all-six-case, all-larger passing training-size suffix of at least two sizes.
No lock is a terminal architecture stop.

### N2: tiny independent confirmation

Only after N1 locks, run the 18 confirmation shards as one Slurm array:
six cases by three independent seeds.  All must pass the locked training size
without retuning.  A pure merger publishes the tiny certificate last.

### N3: PARIS engineering preflight

Only after the tiny certificate:

1. authenticate the calibrated input and spectrum;
2. construct moderate \(q=256,512\) prefix/replay cases;
3. measure construction, training, scoring, score-gradient, and artifact
   resources;
4. run a true one-epoch \(q=512\), batch-1024, score-microbatch-64 profile
   through one optimizer update;
5. require exact fixed-\(P\) replay and bounded memory; and
6. freeze one shared-node Slurm resource request before PARIS fits.

Resource measurements may set only Slurm time and memory.  They do not select
a scientific rank or change the batch, microbatch, architecture, or
objective.

### N4: PARIS development

Run the four homogeneous \(q\times S\) tasks as one Slurm array.  Each task
fits both initializations.  Require:

- all finite normalized likelihoods and score derivatives;
- model-selection-versus-test NLL difference no larger than
  `max(0.02*q nat, 5*pooled NLL MCSE)`;
- model-selection-versus-test observation-score and mass-score risks agreeing
  within five pooled MCSE;
- paired per-draw mass-score squared error lower than the exact Gaussian
  moment-closure baseline by more than five standard errors on both
  independent domains;
- paired observation-score squared error over the first 32 coordinates lower
  than the Gaussian baseline by more than five standard errors on both
  independent domains;
- percentage score-risk improvements reported as diagnostics rather than
  thresholds;
- simulator mean/covariance agreement with analytic moments;
- coordinate, selected-pair, and radial simulator checks under the calibrated
  G4 marginal/tail thresholds;
- nested-\(S\) flow likelihood differences no larger than 0.05 nat median and
  0.20 nat at the 99th percentile on the frozen reporting grid;
- the same 0.05/0.20-nat limits for complete \(q=256\)-versus-\(512\) hybrid
  likelihoods; and
- exact translation parity and no realized-`mf` access.

Both \(q\) values and both \(S\) values must pass.  The locked controls are
\(q=256,S=65536\) only if the entire two-by-two suffix passes; otherwise N4
stops.

### N5: PARIS independent-source confirmation

Only after N4 locks, run seeds 1877, 4099, and 8317 as one Slurm array using
the locked controls.  Each single locked artifact must pass finiteness,
model-selection-versus-test NLL/score agreement, paired score improvement,
simulator moment/tail, replay, and translation gates.  Nested-\(S\) and
cross-\(q\) gates are not repeated because the confirmation tasks fit only
the locked controls.

Across the four development/confirmation source seeds, require:

- likelihood range at most 0.05 nat median and 0.20 nat at the 99th
  percentile; and
- fixed-\(y\), log-mass-score range at most 0.05 median and 0.20 at the 99th
  percentile.

### N6: synthetic calibration

Only after N5, generate 1,024 independent prior-predictive synthetic
datasets from the native simulator.  Infer the scalar root mass on a fixed
deterministic grid using the learned likelihood and the exact calibrated
Gamma prior.  Require:

- maximum empirical rank-CDF deviation at most 0.05;
- absolute coverage error at the 50%, 80%, and 95% central intervals at most
  0.05;
- no systematic posterior-mean bias larger than 0.05 prior SD; and
- replay under an independent simulation seed.

This is a synthetic calibration experiment, not a claim of ground truth for
the Lunt-like CH4 observations.

## Operational rules

- Commit and push every scientific or executable source change before use.
- Resolve and record full Git SHAs; use fresh detached full-SHA worktrees and
  fresh run roots.
- Use one Slurm array for homogeneous repeated tasks.
- Use ordinary shared nodes unless a measured resource need justifies

  only a short launch-settling check.

- Do not open the protected catalogue or write to `PARIS_inversions`.

## Interpretation boundary

Passing this plan would certify one normalized approximation to the calibrated
root-only projected marginal and its retained-mass derivative on declared
synthetic domains.  It would not make the Gaussian complement exact, prove
portability to another operator or partition, or license data-dependent
partition weights.  Those require separate tests.
