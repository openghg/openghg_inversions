openghg_inversions.rhime#

Public RHIME runners, specifications, builders, and orchestration stages.

Use run_rhime, run_rhime_multisector, run_rhime_nested, or the prepared-input entry points for complete runs. Copied runners may use the supported resolve, retrieve/reload, filter, basis, sensitivity, assembly, alignment, materialization, build, sample, result, and output stages directly. Alignment is pure; acquisition may access data, model materialization crosses the eager backend boundary, sampling executes PyMC, and output stages may write requested products.

Bases: object

Prepared outer, inner, and combined nested-domain inputs.

outer and inner retain their native basis operators and flux grids. combined retains the outer basis for the ordinary RHIME validation contract and adds the aligned inner sensitivity as H_inner. The inner basis remains available explicitly on inner and is never coerced onto the outer grid. outer_overlap_mask_policy records how that support was removed so saved per-domain artifacts remain scientifically interpretable.

Parameters:

  • outer (RhimePreparedInputs) – Prepared outer-domain inputs with the inner-domain spatial response removed before basis projection.

  • inner (RhimePreparedInputs) – Prepared inner-domain inputs on their native grid.

  • combined (RhimePreparedInputs) – Observation-aligned inputs containing outer H and inner H_inner sensitivities.

  • time_tolerance (str | Timedelta | None) – Maximum time difference allowed when aligning inner observations to the outer observation index.

  • inner_state_dim (str) – State dimension assigned to the inner sensitivity.

  • inner_domain_label (str | None) – Optional label retained for the inner domain.

  • outer_overlap_masked (bool) – Whether the caller confirms that the outer response was masked before basis projection.

  • outer_overlap_mask_policy (str) – Machine-readable description of how the overlapping outer response was removed.

build_co2_model(
flux_sensitivity,
*,
retained_prior,
fixed_prior_contribution,
observations,
observation_error,
aggregation_error,
likelihood_builder=None,
likelihood_kwargs=None,
sigma_alignment=None,
sigma_prior=None,
fixed_model_mismatch=None,
state_activity=None,
boundary_sensitivity=None,
bc_prior=None,
bc_state_activity=None,
offset_prior=None,
offset_args=None,
)#

Build the CO2 coherent-reduction model from explicit scientific arrays.

flux_sensitivity is prepared once as the reduced operator H_alpha: exact-zero columns are omitted from the backend co2_sensitivity while flux_scaling retains the complete labelled scientific state. fixed_prior_contribution is then added with the shared coherent-affine component to produce the complete co2_flux_contribution. Optional boundary and offset terms are added afterwards to produce modelled_concentration.

fixed_prior_contribution is the affine term H m - H_alpha (Pi m). The latter is a fixed prior contribution, not an atmospheric boundary condition. retained_prior contains the complete labelled arithmetic moments for the positive flux state.

Known fixed states remain in the public state and forward calculation but are omitted from the sampled correlated state. fixed_model_mismatch is an optional known concentration standard deviation. openghg_inversions leaves this policy unset by default; the Verification Games fixed likelihood passes 1 ppm explicitly. Inner and outer same-grid states remain in this one flux state and are distinguished by basis_group metadata retained for output-side selection. The model builds only their complete shared flux contribution. Optional boundary and offset terms remain scientifically distinct components named mu_bc and offset.

Direct custom callers are responsible for supplying scientifically coherent arrays from one preparation and for their positional semantics when labels are absent.

Parameters:

  • flux_sensitivity (DataArray) – Reduced CO2 sensitivity with observation dimension nmeasure and one labelled retained-state dimension.

  • retained_prior (CorrelatedLognormalPrior) – Complete labelled arithmetic-moment prior for the retained positive state.

  • fixed_prior_contribution (DataArray) – Fixed coherent-reduction affine intercept named fixed_prior_contribution on nmeasure.

  • observations (DataArray) – Observed CO2 concentrations on nmeasure.

  • observation_error (DataArray) – Reported observation standard deviation.

  • aggregation_error (AggregationError) – Prepared fixed aggregation-error representation.

  • likelihood_builder (Callable[[...], TensorVariable] | None) – Optional ordinary likelihood component. When supplied, it replaces the default additive-sigma likelihood.

  • likelihood_kwargs (Mapping[str, Any] | None) – Options passed only to the selected likelihood.

  • sigma_alignment (SigmaAlignment | None) – Optional grouping policy for inferred additive model error.

  • sigma_prior (dict[str, Any] | None) – Optional prior arguments for inferred additive model error.

  • fixed_model_mismatch (float | DataArray | None) – Optional known scalar or labelled concentration standard deviation.

  • state_activity (StateActivity | None) – Optional labelled activity policy for retained flux states.

  • boundary_sensitivity (DataArray | None) – Optional atmospheric boundary-condition sensitivity already resolved for the model, for example data.

  • bc_prior (dict[str, Any] | None) – Optional prior arguments for boundary-condition scaling.

  • bc_state_activity (StateActivity | None) – Optional labelled activity policy for boundary states.

  • offset_prior (dict[str, Any] | None) – Optional prior for an offset component. When omitted, no offset is added. Site codes are derived from the site coordinate on observations.

  • offset_args (Mapping[str, Any] | None) – Optional offset settings: offset_freq, drop_first, and per_site.

Returns:
