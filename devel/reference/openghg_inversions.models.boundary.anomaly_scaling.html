openghg_inversions.models.boundary.anomaly_scaling#

Boundary corrections that scale the prescribed concentration anomaly.

Centered sensitivity (BoundCenteredSensitivity; see Boundary-condition operators) gives the prescribed boundary as A 1 + c M 1. This family adds two corrections: a mean shift m in concentration units, propagated through M as in the additive family, and dimensionless independent zero-mean Gaussian scalings v of the reference-adjusted anomaly A' on each (bc_curtain, bc_period) state:

m M 1 + A’ v

A' is the anomaly about a chosen reference c'. Changing the reference leaves the prescribed concentration unchanged but changes A', and hence the covariance of A' v: the reference is part of this prior, and this is the only boundary family where it has scientific meaning. Explicitly, A' = A + (c - c') M, so A' 1 + c' M 1 = A 1 + c M 1.

class AnomalyScalingBoundary(choice, data)#

Bases: CenteredBoundary

Anomaly-scaling choice bound to one fit’s borrowed centered operands.

Variables:

  • choice (openghg_inversions.models.boundary.anomaly_scaling.AnomalyScalingChoice) – The resolved anomaly-scaling choice.

  • data (openghg_inversions.models.boundary.contracts.BoundCenteredSensitivity) – The recentred H_bc_anomaly A' (concentration units) and H_bc_mass (dimensionless) on (observation, bc_region), where bc_region indexes (bc_curtain, bc_period) states, and the chosen scalar bc_reference c' in the anomaly’s units. The arrays are borrowed and may be Dask-backed. Materialize them jointly with the other model inputs before add(); rows may be padded or subset with dataclasses.replace, keeping that layout.

AnomalyScalingChoice.bind() establishes data by construction, so the record trusts that contract rather than validating it again.

add(*, output_dim='nmeasure', prefix='', owner='bc')#

Add the mean shift, anomaly scalings and boundary concentration to the current model.

Must be called inside an active PyMC model that has the coordinate registry, after data has been materialized jointly with the other model inputs. It registers the sensitivity, reference, scale and any period indicators as model data, plus the sampled variables and their deterministic responses.

Parameters:

  • output_dim (str) – Observation dimension registered by the model.

  • prefix (str) – Prefix for graph variable names.

  • owner (str | None) – Namespace for component-owned boundary-state coordinates; None keeps the local names such as bc_region. Graph names are controlled separately by prefix.

Returns:

output is the absolute boundary concentration mu_bc; the likelihood contribution omits the fixed c' M 1, which is the reference shift; the fixed contribution is A' 1; the affine blocks are the mean response through M (when sampled) followed by A' for the scalings v; and the sampled variables are the mean latent and v.

Return type:

The boundary term

Raises:

  • ValueError – If output_dim is not the bound observation dimension, data is still lazy, materialized operands are invalid, or a whole-window mean prior is not scalar.

  • RuntimeError – If the model lacks the coordinate registry.

class AnomalyScalingChoice(
mean_prior,
scale_sd,
reference='stored',
mean_by_period=False,
frequency=None,
anchor_time=None,
)#

Bases: object

Prior specification for anomaly-scaling boundary corrections, before binding.

A choice holds the scientific options (mean shift prior, scaling width, reference policy and boundary period calendar) without bound observations or sensitivity arrays. bind() pairs it with one fit’s observations and centered sensitivity.

Variables:

  • mean_prior (collections.abc.Mapping[str, Any] | None) – Prior for the mean shift in observation concentration units, scalar or one shift per period with mean_by_period; or None for no mean shift.

  • scale_sd (float) – Finite positive standard deviation of the dimensionless anomaly scalings.

  • reference (float | Literal['stored', 'whole_window_transport_mean']) – "stored" keeps the prepared reference; a finite number in observation concentration units replaces it; and "whole_window_transport_mean" chooses the reference for which the fit’s recentred anomaly sums to zero over all rows, c + sum(A) / sum(M).

  • mean_by_period (bool) – Fit one independent mean shift per boundary period; requires a mean prior and a frequency.

  • frequency (str | None) – Boundary period frequency, with the syntax and calendar of bc_freq; see AdditiveChoice.

  • anchor_time (str | datetime.datetime | numpy.datetime64 | pandas._libs.tslibs.timestamps.Timestamp | None) – Origin of fixed-duration periods; None uses the earliest selected observation of the fit. See AdditiveChoice.

Raises:

ValueError – On construction, for a non-positive or non-finite scale, an unknown or non-finite reference, mean_by_period without a mean prior or frequency, or an invalid frequency or anchor.

property mean_shift: MeanShift#

The mean shift these options define; without a prior it adds nothing.

bind(observations, sensitivity)#

Bind centered sensitivity to one fit and re-express it about the chosen reference.

Parameters:

  • observations (DataArray) – One channel’s observations with its observation axis first. Its labels select and order the sensitivity rows; its time values assign rows to boundary periods.

  • sensitivity (RawCenteredSensitivity) – Raw centered sensitivity on its nmeasure or time axis. Centering weights are ignored.

Returns:

The bound boundary. H_bc_anomaly is the recentred A' and H_bc_mass is M, both on (observation, bc_region) with a (bc_curtain, bc_period) state index; bc_reference is the chosen scalar c'. A whole-window reference is computed from the rows of this fit. Payloads, the recentred anomaly and any computed reference stay lazy until joint materialization.

Raises:

ValueError – If PyTensor is not configured for float64, or periods or indexed timestamps conflict with the observations.

Return type:

AnomalyScalingBoundary
