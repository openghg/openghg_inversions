openghg_inversions.coherent_reduction#

Exact labelled Gaussian reduction from one native model.

For x ~ N(m, B) and alpha = Pi x, the reduced conditional observation model is

y | alpha ~ N(H m + H_alpha (alpha - Pi m), R + A),

where H_alpha = H B Pi.T C_alpha^-1 and A = H B H.T - H_alpha C_alpha H_alpha.T. The public function constructs all linked covariance products from the same B/H/Pi inputs before applying these equations.

The same reduction splits a native state as x = m + U_* (alpha - Pi m) + x_perp, with U_* = B Pi.T C_alpha^-1 and x_perp independent of alpha with covariance B_perp = B - U_* C_alpha U_*.T. For a labelled native linear functional Q, such as a country total, project_native_functionals() returns the products needed to condition Q x_perp on the data as well as on alpha. Without them a functional reports only Q m + D (alpha - Pi m).

Reduction-side products for labelled native linear functionals Q.

With x = m + U_* (alpha - Pi m) + x_perp, a functional splits as Q x = Q m + D (alpha - Pi m) + Q x_perp with D = Q U_*. Given alpha, any other model parameters beta and the data y, Q x_perp is Gaussian with

  • mean Q B_perp H.T (A + R)^-1 r, where r = y - y_0 - H_alpha (alpha - Pi m) - G beta is the draw’s residual;

  • covariance Q B_perp Q.T - Q B_perp H.T (A + R)^-1 H B_perp Q.T.

Under the prior, Q x_perp is independent of alpha with covariance Q B_perp Q.T. For a Gaussian retained state and the exact A, the resulting functional mean and covariance equal the native-grid Gaussian posterior for any retained partition. With a LogNormal retained state the conditioning on each draw is still exact under the reduced model, but the reduced model itself is the moment-matched approximation described under “Assumptions and limitations” in the coherent-reduction guide.

Variables:

  • functional_mean (xarray.core.dataarray.DataArray) – Q m on the functional axis, in the functional’s units.

  • retained_response (xarray.core.dataarray.DataArray) – D = Q U_* on (functional_dim, state_dim). A state marginalised into A has a zero column.

  • unresolved_observation_covariance (xarray.core.dataarray.DataArray) – Q B_perp H.T on (functional_dim, observation_dim).

  • unresolved_covariance (xarray.core.dataarray.DataArray) – Positive-semidefinite Q B_perp Q.T on distinct functional row and column axes.

  • retained_covariance (xarray.core.dataarray.DataArray) – C_alpha of the full-restriction reduction the products were computed for. It identifies that reduction and is needed to marginalise states later.

  • effective_observation_operator (xarray.core.dataarray.DataArray) – H_alpha of that reduction, with the sensitivity units the residuals must use.

  • reduction_identity (str) – reduction_identity() of that reduction. It depends on the native observation operator H, the covariance B, the restriction Pi and the native mean, which Q m and D alone do not identify.

  • marginalised (xarray.core.dataarray.DataArray | None) – Boolean state mask of states already moved into the unresolved part by marginalise_functional_states(), or None for full-restriction products.

reduction_identity(
*,
native_identity,
retained_covariance,
effective_observation_operator,
unresolved_observation_covariance,
)#

Return the identity of one coherent reduction.

Different native problems can share C_alpha, H_alpha and A: if one observation sees either of two exchangeable cells, swapping the cells leaves all three unchanged but reverses the covariance between a cell’s functional and the observation. The identity therefore combines the native_reduction_identity() of the native inputs with a hash of the three reduced arrays. Anyone holding the native identity and the stored arrays, such as a prepared artifact, can recompute it, so a stored A or H_alpha that was changed after reduction no longer matches.

Arrays are hashed as float64 values in their stored axis order together with their index labels (not dimension names), so the identity survives dimension renaming. It is an exact digest, not a tolerance-based comparison.

Parameters:

  • native_identity (str) – native_reduction_identity() of the native inputs, as recorded by the reduction.

  • retained_covariance (DataArray) – C_alpha.

  • effective_observation_operator (DataArray) – H_alpha.

  • unresolved_observation_covariance (DataArray) – The exact dense A.

Returns:

"sha256:<hex digest>".

Raises:

ValueError – If native_identity is not a native identity digest.

Return type:

str

native_reduction_identity(*, covariance, products, native_mean, native_sensitivity)#

Return the identity of the native inputs of one coherent reduction.

For the built-in separable covariance and independent combinations of those covariances, this hashes the complete covariance description, native sensitivity H, restriction Pi, native mean m, and prolongation U_* with their index labels. Sparse payloads stay sparse. Other covariance actions receive a fresh identity on each call because their unobserved covariance blocks cannot be inferred from the reduced arrays. Keep the identity issued when the fit and its products are reduced together.

Parameters:

  • covariance (InvertibleNativeCovarianceAction) – The native covariance action B.

  • products (NativeCovarianceProducts) – Native covariance products for B, H and Pi.

  • native_mean (DataArray) – Native mean m on the covariance’s native dims.

  • native_sensitivity (DataArray) – Native H.

Returns:

"sha256:<hex digest>".

Return type:

str

Prepare the exact centred Gaussian model for one B/H/Pi/m set.

This is a named eager numerical boundary. native_mean and native_sensitivity may be Dask-backed; their payloads are materialized together so a shared graph is executed once. The strategy then selects and materializes Pi from its own inputs. Inputs are borrowed and are not mutated.

The inputs must use the covariance action’s native dimensions and carry exactly matching indexed coordinates on shared dimensions. Unit conversion belongs to the upstream OpenGHG/pint-xarray preparation boundary: m and Pi describe dimensionless scaling, while H is already expressed in the desired observation units.

Parameters:

  • covariance (InvertibleNativeCovarianceAction) – Invertible labelled native covariance action B.

  • strategy (RetainedProjectionStrategy) – Authoritative retained restriction strategy, owning the inputs that define Pi.

  • state_dim (str) – Retained-state dimension of Pi.

  • native_mean (DataArray) – Dimensionless native scaling mean m.

  • native_sensitivity (DataArray) – Native sensitivity H in canonical observation units.

  • observation_dim (str) – Observation dimension in native_sensitivity.

  • observation_batch_size (int) – Covariance right-hand-side batch size.

  • functionals (DataArray | None) – Optional native linear functionals Q on (functional_dim, *native_dims), in the reporting units per unit scaling. See project_native_functionals().

  • functional_dim (str) – Functional row dimension of functionals.

Returns:

The retained prior, centred effective forward model, and unresolved observation covariance, plus the functional products when functionals is given.

Raises:

ValueError – If xarray cannot transpose or exactly align the labelled inputs, or if covariance-product construction fails.

Return type:

CoherentGaussianReduction

project_native_functionals(
*,
covariance,
products,
functionals,
functional_dim,
native_mean,
native_sensitivity,
observation_dim,
batch_size=64,
native_identity=None,
)#

Compute the coherent-reduction products of native linear functionals.

This is a named eager numerical boundary. B is applied to bounded blocks of functional rows, B Q.T, and each block is contracted with H and Q at once, so neither B nor the full B Q.T is held. A sparse or Dask-backed Q is computed once and stays sparse; only one row block at a time is densified. Inputs are borrowed.

A functional that should be reported jointly, such as one country total per source, needs one row per term so that the joint covariance across rows is available; sums of rows are then reconstructed per draw.

Parameters:

  • covariance (InvertibleNativeCovarianceAction) – The native covariance action B used for products.

  • products (NativeCovarianceProducts) – Dense-view products from project_native_covariance() for the same B, H and Pi.

  • functionals (DataArray) – Native functionals Q on (functional_dim, *native_dims) with a unique functional index. Optional units describe one functional per unit scaling.

  • functional_dim (str) – Functional row dimension.

  • native_mean (DataArray) – Dimensionless native mean m, exactly aligned with the covariance grid.

  • native_sensitivity (DataArray) – The native sensitivity H used for products.

  • observation_dim (str) – Observation dimension of H.

  • batch_size (int) – Positive number of functional rows per covariance block.

  • native_identity (str | None) – native_reduction_identity() of these inputs when the caller has already computed it, as reduce_native_gaussian() does; otherwise it is computed here.

Returns:

Q m, D = Q U_*, Q B_perp H.T and Q B_perp Q.T.

Raises:

ValueError – If labels or dimensions do not match, the products are not dense-view, or Q holds non-finite values.

Return type:

NativeFunctionalProducts

marginalise_functional_states(products, marginalised)#

Move marginalised retained states of functional products into x_perp.

Integrating states M out of a full-restriction reduction gives the coherent reduction with the kept rows K of Pi. With G = C_MK C_KK^-1 and S = C_MM - G C_KM, the products become D'_K = D_K + D_M G, D'_M = 0, Q B'_perp H.T = Q B_perp H.T + D_M S H_M.T and Q B'_perp Q.T = Q B_perp Q.T + D_M S D_M.T, where H_M are the marginalised columns of H_alpha. Marginalised states’ functional contribution then goes through the unresolved path, consistently with an A that absorbed H_M S H_M.T. A reduction that marginalises states into A must apply this to its functional products as well. Inputs are borrowed.

Parameters:

  • products (NativeFunctionalProducts) – Full-restriction functional products, carrying that reduction’s C_alpha and H_alpha.

  • marginalised (DataArray) – Boolean mask on the retained-state axis; True marks a state to integrate out.

Returns:

Products for the kept-state reduction on the full state axis, so a marginalised state held at its prior mean contributes nothing through D, with marginalised recorded. Unchanged products when no state is marginalised.

Raises:

ValueError – If the products were already marginalised, state labels differ, or every state is marginalised.

Return type:

NativeFunctionalProducts
