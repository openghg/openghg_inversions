openghg_inversions.models.boundary.centered#

Shared base and graph pieces of the centered boundary families.

Centered sensitivity represents the absolute boundary sensitivity as A + c M (BoundCenteredSensitivity). The centered multiplicative, additive and anomaly-scaling families inherit CenteredBoundary and build their boundary term in the same order:

  1. CenteredBoundary._register_sensitivity() checks the materialized sensitivity once and registers A, M and c, returning a RegisteredSensitivity;

  2. the family adds its corrections in scientific order, each a Correction; the additive and anomaly-scaling families start with the shared MeanShift;

  3. CenteredBoundary._term() adds the absolute mu_bc and returns the AffineConcentrationTerm.

CenteredBoundary._outputs() declares the matching shared outputs. The base defines no add, so each family’s recipe stays explicit. Absolute multiplicative sensitivity has no centered representation and does not use these pieces.

The additive and anomaly-scaling families share one mean equation: a shift m in observation concentration units on every bound boundary state, either one scalar over the inversion window or one independent value per boundary period, contributing M m to the observations. Under supported surface preparation M 1 = 1; otherwise the response is the propagated shift rather than a constant.

class Correction(state, signal, block, latent)#

Bases: object

Graph pieces of one sampled boundary correction, such as a mean shift or anomalies.

Variables:

  • state (pytensor.tensor.variable.TensorVariable) – Correction on every bound boundary state. Its units depend on the formulation: concentration units for mean shifts and additive anomalies, which respond through M; dimensionless for anomaly scalings v and multiplicative deviations b - 1, which respond through A' and A + c M. Combine states only within one formulation.

  • signal (pytensor.tensor.variable.TensorVariable) – Observation response of the correction, in concentration units.

  • block (openghg_inversions.models.components.AffineBlock) – Affine block: the response to each sampled coefficient, with those physical coefficients.

  • latent (pytensor.tensor.variable.TensorVariable) – Sampled variable behind the correction, after any reparameterization.

class MeanShift(prior, by_period=False)#

Bases: object

A shift m on every bound boundary state, in observation concentration units.

Variables:

  • prior (collections.abc.Mapping[str, Any] | None) – Mean shift prior, as passed to parse_prior(), or None for no shift. With by_period, labelled parameters may lie on bc_mean_period with the fit’s period-start labels; they are qualified with the coordinate owner like the period indicators.

  • by_period (bool) – Sample one independent shift per boundary period instead of one scalar over the inversion window.
