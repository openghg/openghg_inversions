Boundary correction structures#

Centered boundary sensitivity gives the prescribed boundary concentration as \(A\mathbf1+cM\mathbf1\) (see Boundary-condition operators). Two boundary families correct it with independent zero-mean Gaussian coefficients whose widths are explicit scientific inputs; nothing is calibrated from the data.

  • AdditiveChoice adds concentration-unit state corrections \(Md\). Its anomalies option states the structure of \(d\).

  • AnomalyScalingChoice adds \(\delta M\mathbf1+A'v\), scaling the prescribed anomaly by dimensionless \(v\).

Both are choices: pass one as boundary= to a CO2 frontend, or one per channel to a linked CO2/O2 frontend. The frontend binds it to the prepared centered operands of that fit; no correction artifact is prepared or stored. The options are recorded in the trace metadata and the arrays the graph uses in its constant data.

Additive structures#

States \(j\) are boundary curtains \(k\) and periods \(p\). With \(K\) curtains and \(P\) periods:

\(P(K-1)\)

\(KP\)

none

Every \(z\) and \(u\) coordinate has standard deviation \(s\). A None mean prior omits \(\delta\) from period contrasts or independent anomalies; weighted centering always keeps it. mean_by_period=True replaces \(\delta\mathbf1\) with one independent shift per boundary period, drawn from the same mean prior. frequency and anchor_time follow bc_freq: None gives one period, "monthly" calendar months, and a fixed duration such as "7D" periods anchored at anchor_time or, when it is None, at the fit’s earliest observation (the RHIME parameter adapter defaults bc_freq_anchor to the configured start date). Weighted centering requires the prepared bc_centering_weights; the other structures ignore them.

The mean shift responds through \(M\), contributing \(\delta M\mathbf1\) to the observations. Under supported surface preparation \(M\mathbf1=\mathbf1\); for other transport it is the transported shift rather than a constant.

Temporal curtain contrasts and weighted centering are different priors. Contrasts leave each period’s equal-weight curtain mean to the mean shift; weighted centering constrains only one weighted mean over all states and lets whole periods move together. Independent anomalies with scale \(s\) give the same covariance as curtain contrasts with scale \(s\) plus one mean shift per period (mean_by_period=True) with scale \(s/\sqrt K\), and no other widths. A single whole-window mean gives that covariance only with one period; with several it adds a cross-period covariance \(s^2/K\) that independent anomalies do not have.

Independent anomalies are sampled directly as one coordinate per state, so their design is \(M\) itself and no basis is stored.

Anomaly scaling#

The anomaly-scaling family adds \(\delta M\mathbf1+A'v\): a mean shift transported through \(M\), and dimensionless \(v_j\sim N(0,s^2)\) scaling the reference-adjusted anomaly \(A'\) on each state. A None mean prior omits \(\delta\). Gaussian \(v\) permits negative \(1+v\); no truncation is applied.

\(A'\) is the anomaly about a chosen reference \(c'\). Changing the reference leaves the prescribed concentration unchanged but changes \(A'\), and therefore the covariance of \(A'v\), so the reference is part of the prior. Explicitly, \(A'=A+(c-c')M\) and \(A'\mathbf1+c'M\mathbf1=A\mathbf1+cM\mathbf1\). The options are:

  • reference="stored" keeps the prepared \(c\);

  • a number in observation concentration units sets \(c'\) directly;

  • reference="whole_window_transport_mean" uses \(c'=c+\sum A/\sum M\) over the fit’s rows, so the recentred anomaly sums to zero.

The reference is computed when the choice is bound, from the rows of that fit.

Example#

from openghg_inversions.models.boundary import AdditiveChoice, AnomalyScalingChoice

contrasts = AdditiveChoice(
    {"pdf": "normal", "mu": 0.0, "sigma": 0.5}, 0.3,
    anomalies="period_contrasts", frequency="monthly",
)
scaling = AnomalyScalingChoice(
    {"pdf": "normal", "mu": 0.0, "sigma": 0.5}, 0.8,
    reference="whole_window_transport_mean",
)
# boundary=contrasts for one channel, or
# boundary={"co2": contrasts, "o2": scaling} for a linked fit.

Prior covariance#

Each bound boundary returns an AffineConcentrationTerm whose affine blocks are the observation-space designs \(D_k\) of its Gaussian coefficient groups. With block widths \(s_k\), the prior covariance of the correction is \(\sum_k s_k^2D_kD_k^\mathsf{T}\). A linear functional \(l\) of the observations, such as a row mean or a difference of two rows, has standard deviation \(\sqrt{\sum_k s_k^2\|D_k^\mathsf{T}l\|^2}\). Use this when choosing widths; it is not applied automatically. Before model construction, anomaly_factor() of a bound boundary returns the anomaly design scaled to standard-normal coordinates: \(sMB\) on bc_contrast for weighted anomalies and period contrasts, \(sM\) on bc_region for independent anomalies, and \(sA'\) on bc_region for anomaly scaling.
