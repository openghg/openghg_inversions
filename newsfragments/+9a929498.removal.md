The next minor release resets staged RHIME setup, manifest and scientific-identity contracts. Standard, multisector and CO2 preparation, prior-predictive, sampling and saved-output replay now require their explicit authenticated version-3 manifest contract; historical standard/multisector graph-recovery replay is removed. Independent diagnosis still authenticates historical version-1/2/3 sample envelopes against the posterior digest. The filtered `merged-data/merged-data.nc` checkpoint and its manifest entries are removed. Standalone numerical prepared-input/posterior formats, scientific Python APIs, product schemas and the optional pre-filter acquisition cache remain supported. Standard/multisector preparation now retains valid requested-site subsets after acquisition, compatible cache reload or filtering, aligns per-site choices, and rejects an empty retained set consistently with full execution. RHIME sampling rejects `coords` and `dims` overrides in `sample_kwargs["idata_kwargs"]`; register coordinates and dimensions on the model instead.
