The next minor release resets staged RHIME setup, manifest and scientific-identity contracts. Standard, multisector and CO2 stages now require their explicit authenticated version-3 manifest contract; historical standard/multisector graph-recovery replay is removed. The filtered `merged-data/merged-data.nc` checkpoint and its manifest entries are removed. Standalone numerical prepared-input/posterior formats, scientific Python APIs, product schemas and the optional pre-filter acquisition cache remain supported. Standard/multisector preparation now retains valid requested-site subsets after acquisition, compatible cache reload or filtering, aligns per-site choices, and rejects an empty retained set consistently with full execution.
