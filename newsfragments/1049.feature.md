Add `StateActivity(min_information=...)` and `marginalise_low_information_states` to integrate low-information coherent-reduction states into the unresolved covariance `A` before sampling. The threshold can apply to all states or to named state groups, such as CO2-ocean and O2-ocean sources. The CO2 handoff records each state's information, the marginalised states and the change in `diag A`. Marginalised regions receive no posterior update.
