Add `StateActivity(min_information=...)` and `marginalise_low_information_states` to integrate low-information coherent-reduction states into the unresolved covariance `A` before sampling. The threshold can apply to all states or to named state groups, such as CO2-ocean and O2-ocean sources. The CO2 handoff stores the full-restriction reduction together with each state's information, the marginalised states and the change in `diag A`; `Co2PreparedInputs.fitted_inputs` derives the fitted operator, intercept and `A` after loading with `marginalisation_terms` (a low-rank `A` gains the marginalisation term as exact extra factor columns), and the CO2 runners, concentration reconstruction and complete country outputs use it. Marginalised regions receive no posterior update; complete country outputs include their variance exactly.
