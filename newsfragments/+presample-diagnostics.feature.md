Add `openghg_inversions.presample_diagnostics` for checking a prior before sampling: noise calibration against the de-meaned model-data mismatch per site and network, prior predictive component spreads, per-state prior signal in observation units, and each country's native prior variance split into the part the retained states carry and the part moved into the unresolved covariance `A`. `assess_*` functions grade the results as pass, warn or fail against configurable thresholds. Countries are chosen through the weights passed in, for example from `Countries(country_selections=..., country_regions=...)`.
