`openghg_inversions.models.boundary.CenteredOperands` is removed. Pass a `RawCenteredTransport` Dataset (with optional `bc_centering_weights`) to `choice.bind`, or call `BoundCenteredTransport.bind` directly, instead of `CenteredOperands.from_dataset(...)` and its `bind` method.
`Co2O2PreparedInputs.bc_centering_weights` and the `{channel}_boundary_weights` artifact group are removed: `prepare_co2_o2_inputs` stores each channel's weights in that channel's `boundary_operands`, and the CO2/O2 prepared-input schema version is now 5. Prepare older CO2/O2 inputs again. `BoundaryChoice` no longer takes a transport type parameter.
