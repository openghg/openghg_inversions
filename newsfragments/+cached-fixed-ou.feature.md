Fixed fixed-OU amplitudes are now a specialisation of the cached fixed-OU sampler. Configured CO2 and linked CO2/O2 fits, the saved linked `sample` phase and `run_rhime_co2_o2_from_prepared_inputs` with `tau_hours` and `fixed_site_amplitudes` build the exact state-likelihood quadratic once for the fixed amplitudes and sample the states with stock NUTS on the model priors plus that quadratic, instead of plain NUTS that rebuilt the whitened low-rank factor and its Cholesky factor on every gradient. Fixed amplitudes are stored in `constant_data["ou_site_amplitude"]` (with `ou_units` for mixed-unit linked fits), `target_accept` (or `state_target_accept`) tunes the state step, `sigma_target_accept` is rejected, and at least one active state is required. The cached builders and `run_rhime_co2_o2_cached_sigma_from_prepared_inputs` accept `fixed_site_amplitudes`, and `make_cached_sigma_compound_step` accepts `sigma=None`.
