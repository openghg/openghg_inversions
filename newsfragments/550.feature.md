Add an outer-flux option to the CO2 recipes: `OuterFluxChoice` centres sampled outer-region flux columns on equal-site row weights and holds other outer states at their prior mean, so a centred boundary's mean shift becomes the model's one atmospheric level. `reconstruct_atmospheric_baseline` reports the boundary and outer flux contributions together. A level-only additive boundary (`anomaly_scale=0`) no longer needs centring weights.
