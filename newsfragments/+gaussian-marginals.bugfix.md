`sampling.method = "gaussian"` now reads the prepared per-state flux marginals (`alpha_prior_marginal`). Normal states keep their prior and are no longer recorded as substituted (`state_prior_substitution = "none"` when every state is Normal); LogNormal states are still replaced by their moment-matched Normal; Weibull and gamma states are refused unless `sampling.substitute_marginals = true`. The trace records each replaced family (`state_prior_substitution`, `state_prior_marginals`, `state_prior_substituted`) and any unused copula `latent_correlation_floor`. Previously every run was labelled `"lognormal→normal (moment-matched)"` and a Weibull or gamma marginal was replaced without notice.
