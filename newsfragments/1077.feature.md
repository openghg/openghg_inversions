Add complete native-functional reconstruction for coherent reductions. `reduce_native_gaussian(functionals=...)` returns `NativeFunctionalProducts` (`Q m`, `D = Q U*`, `Q B_perp H.T` and `Q B_perp Q.T`, with the reduction's `C_alpha` and `H_alpha`) computed from covariance actions. `postprocessing.unresolved_functionals.complete_functional_draws` adds the conditional mean and a conditional-covariance draw of the unresolved native state to each retained draw, with `(A + R)^-1` supplied as a labelled dense or fixed-OU low-rank solve. `co2_country_flux_outputs(..., unresolved=...)` uses it, checks that the products match the map's partition and the fit's inactive states, and labels its outputs `uncertainty_scope="complete_native"`; without it, outputs stay `retained_state_conditional`. `co2_country_functionals` builds the matching country functionals, country membership may vary by source, and `marginalise_functional_states` moves marginalised states into the unresolved part.
