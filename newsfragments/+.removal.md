In 0.8, remove the direct ``fixedbasisMCMC`` and ``inferpymc`` implementation,
its fixed-basis preparation and model helpers, and the
``--legacy-fixedbasis`` option. The 0.7.x line is the last release line with
direct HBMCMC execution. Existing supported fixedbasis-style INI files can
continue through ``run_hbmcmc.py``, which now always translates them to the
RHIME workflow; ``output_format="legacy"`` remains available for modern
HBMCMC-compatible NetCDF output.

Merged-data mappings no longer repeat species, observation-unit scale, or
site calibration scales in the unused ``.species``, ``.units``, and
``.scales`` entries. Existing artifacts containing those entries remain
loadable. The compatibility wrapper also stops recording the removed
``additive_sigma_likelihood_builder`` callback as if it were still active.
