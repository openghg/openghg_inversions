`CorrelatedPrior` takes an opt-in `latent_correlation_floor` (`[prepared_inputs.native_prior] latent_correlation_floor` in CO2 configurations) that repairs a copula prior whose latent correlation is not positive definite, such as Weibull GPP/TER marginals on wide ORCHIDEE-based priors. The default still rejects such priors, and a repair that would change any implied state correlation by more than 0.01 is refused. The repair and its effect on the implied covariance are recorded in `latent_correlation_repair` and in the trace's `rhime_model_metadata`, which now also names the prior's marginals; non-lognormal priors are no longer described as lognormal there.
