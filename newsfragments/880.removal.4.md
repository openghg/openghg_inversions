Retire the separate cached CO₂ workflow in favour of model-driven `run_co2`
and shared CO₂ handoffs. Migration:

- Replace `run_rhime_co2_cached_sigma`, `rhime.run(model="co2_cached_sigma")`,
  `Co2RunSetup`, and CO₂ use of `resolve_co2_family_config` with `run_co2`
  (or `rhime.run(model="co2")`). Select `likelihood.kind="fixed_ou"` and
  `site_amplitude_prior`; the returned `Co2Output.idata` contains the trace.
- Remove the CO₂ `variant` key and replace `co2_cached_sigma.toml` with
  `co2_fixed_ou.toml`. Linked CO₂/O₂ retains its separate configuration.
- Replace `resolve_stage_setup`, `load_stage_params`, `prepare_rhime_stage`,
  `sample_rhime_stage`, `postprocess_rhime_stage`, and `prior_predictive_stage`
  with the recipe's handoff APIs or installed phase commands. CO₂ saved
  sampling takes `model` and `sampling` mappings.
- `configuration_identity` and `effective_configuration` are removed:
  preparation generations and saved scientific meaning replace configuration
  hashes. Regenerate historical schema-version-2 sample envelopes through the
  current preparation/sample handoffs; no compatibility reader is provided.
- Replace `sample_co2_cached_prior_predictive` with shared CO₂
  `handoffs.prior_predictive`, or compose construction and PyMC prediction.
  New CO₂ traces use `rhime_recipe="co2"` with separate inference metadata.
- Saved phases no longer accept `--prepared-inputs`; postprocessing also
  removes `--posterior` and `--preparation-manifest`. Use one preparation
  manifest for sampling/readiness and one sample manifest for postprocessing.
  The CO₂ diagnose manifest is removed; diagnosis still accepts `--posterior`
  directly with its check options.
