CO₂ runs and `rhime prepare --model co2` can build a coherent preparation from configured surface acquisition. Preparation checks the requested source set and removes non-finite observation rows before coherent reduction. The full runner uses the prepared inputs and affine companion in memory, and the staged command saves the pair for reuse. The configured boundary choice selects the representation, with an optional explicit request in `prepared_inputs.boundary_representation`. Saved stages reject incompatible representations and weighted additive preparations whose weights do not match the fit calendar.
