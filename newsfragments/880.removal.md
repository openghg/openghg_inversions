In 0.8, multisector staged envelopes and multi-artifact CLI arguments are replaced by `rhime.multisector_stages` preparation/sample manifests. Regenerate historical checkpoints or use their original package version. Full, prepared and saved execution return `InversionOutput`; use its trace, scientific roles and metadata directly.

Replace `run_rhime_from_prepared_inputs` and its multisector adapters with `run_multisector(prepared=..., config=...)`. Replace the raw `build_multisector_rhime_model` with one eager `build_multisector_model(model_inputs, model_spec, priors=...)`; access its graph through `.model`. Whole custom models compose scientific operations directly, without the removed run-specification builder hook. Standard and multisector share `make_model_output(..., split_by_sectors=...)` and `save_inversion_output`, with explicit product computation. Numerical codecs and product formats are preserved; nested/CO2 envelopes await their own migrations.
