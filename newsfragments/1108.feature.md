`prepare_co2_inputs`, `run_co2` and the CO2 `prepare` handoff now choose the low-rank aggregation-error rank from the spectrum of the unresolved covariance whitened by a reference mismatch covariance, instead of a fixed rank of 512. `observation_error.WhitenedRankRule` holds the criteria (keep every mode with whitened eigenvalue above 0.1, optionally more to meet a dropped-mode KL budget, or an explicit rank override that warns when it is below the rule rank). The reference is a separate `reference_covariance` input: `rhime.co2.Co2ReferenceCovariance` settings (reported observation error plus an independent 1 ppm amplitude by default, optionally per-site amplitudes and within-site OU time scales) or a labelled covariance in the canonical observation order with squared mole-fraction units. Provenance `aggregation_error_rank_selection` records the reference description, the criteria, the count above the threshold, the chosen rank and the dropped-mode KL. Re-preparing with supplied provenance no longer carries over earlier aggregation-error diagnostics.
