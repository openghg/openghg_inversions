Linked CO2/O2 prepared inputs now accept low-rank-plus-diagonal joint aggregation error. `prepare_co2_o2_inputs` defaults to rank 512, as on the CO2 route (`aggregation_error_rank=None` keeps the dense matrix), and takes an explicit `aggregation_error_factorization` of `"joint"` or `"own_species"`. It also accepts an explicit, recorded `independent_error_floor` with no default. Preparation can record the Gaussian KL per observation between the dense and approximate total covariance over supplied mismatch amplitudes, computed with the new `aggregation_error_kl_per_observation`. The linked prepared-input schema version is now 6; prepare older artifacts again.
