Added `openghg_inversions.correlated_source_covariance`: `CorrelatedSourceCovariance` and the `gpp_ter_source_covariance(..., gpp_ter_correlation=rho)` option give a multisource native covariance `B = S (R ⊗ K) S` with a GPP–TER correlation `rho` of the native scaling anomalies (GPP negative uptake, so `rho > 0` cancels in net biosphere flux). Passed to `reduce_native_gaussian`, it gives one joint GPP/TER reduction whose retained covariance `C_alpha` and unresolved covariance `A` (dense or low rank) carry the cross-source blocks. Correlated sources must share one kernel (any native covariance action that exposes `sigma` and `to_dataset()`) and need `|rho| <= 1`. The default `rho = 0` is bit-identical to `IndependentSourceCovariance`.
