Saved CO2 boundary traces now store unsampled additive anomalies once as zero `bc_anomaly` and `mu_bc_anomaly` constant data, which output products broadcast over the draws, and `rhime_boundary_format_version` is 2; repeat sampling for older posteriors. `likelihood_representation` now registers `boundary_reference_shift` itself and returns only the likelihood mean and observations. The `operands` and `mean_shift` boundary modules are folded into `contracts`, `multiplicative` and the new `centered` module.

The centered boundary contracts are renamed `RawCenteredSensitivity`, `BoundCenteredSensitivity` and `BoundAdditiveSensitivity`, and choices bind `(observations, sensitivity)`. In `openghg_inversions.models.boundary`, the protocols `BoundaryChoice` and `Boundary` become `Choice` and `Bound`, and `BoundaryOutputs` becomes `Outputs`; these names are no longer re-exported from `openghg_inversions.models`. `MultiplicativeChoice.bind` returns the new `CenteredMultiplicativeBoundary` for centered sensitivity, and `MultiplicativeChoice.bind_absolute` returns `MultiplicativeBoundary` for absolute `H_bc`, which is again added as an ordinary linear component (`hbc` holds only its nonzero columns); recipes bind through `bind_prepared`, and the `centered` property is removed. Centered multiplicative outputs now also declare `mu_bc_reference` and `boundary_mean`. The centered multiplicative, additive and anomaly-scaling families share a `CenteredBoundary` base, with `MeanShift` and `Anomalies` records replacing the paired graph and output functions. Weight checks are the module functions `validate_centering_weights` and `normalize_centering_weights`. Saved boundary decoding moves from `openghg_inversions.rhime.co2.boundary_output` to `openghg_inversions.postprocessing.boundary_outputs`, with `SavedBoundary` and `decode_saved_boundaries` replacing `BoundaryOutputFacts` and `decode_boundary_facts`.
