Per-source marginals for the correlated flux prior. `CorrelatedPrior` now accepts `"normal"`, `"weibull"` and `"gamma"` marginals as well as `"lognormal"`, joined by a Gaussian copula that reproduces the arithmetic mean and covariance exactly. Configured per-source native priors take `marginal = ...` in `[prepared_inputs.native_prior.sources.<source>]`. Preparation stores the choice as `alpha_prior_marginal`, and the ordinary and cached CO2 runners use it. All-lognormal priors build the same model as before.
