Standard and multisector RHIME expose complete resolved ``RhimeConfig`` before data access, with direct preparation fields and public aligned ``SiteOptions``. Construct it with ``RhimeConfig.from_params`` or the separate ``read_rhime_ini`` frontend, then pass ``config=`` to a runner to reuse the resolved request. ``resolve_rhime_config`` remains supported. Invalid ``flux_non_finite_check``, ``aggregation_error_mode``, active ``basis_algorithm`` and named ``min_error`` choices are rejected before acquisition, preserving saved-basis precedence. Shared ``load_rhime_data`` handles supplied, cached and fresh data; ``retrieve_inversion_data`` replaces the deprecated ``data_processing_surface_notracer`` name while preserving its six-tuple return. Requested choices remain distinct from retained run metadata.
