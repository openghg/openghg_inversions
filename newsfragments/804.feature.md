Standard and multisector RHIME expose complete resolved ``RhimeConfig`` before data access, with direct preparation fields, public aligned ``SiteOptions`` and a resolved ``read_rhime_ini`` reader. Invalid ``flux_non_finite_check`` and ``aggregation_error_mode`` choices are rejected before acquisition. Shared ``load_rhime_data`` handles supplied, cached and fresh data; ``retrieve_inversion_data`` replaces the deprecated ``data_processing_surface_notracer`` name while preserving its six-tuple return. Requested choices remain distinct from retained run metadata.
