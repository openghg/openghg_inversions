Surface CO2 and linked CO2/O2 acquisition and preparation accept an opt-in `bc_time_method = "mean_age"` (default `"release_time"`) in `[prepared_inputs.acquisition]`, `MergedData.from_options`, `acquire_surface_data` and `prepare_surface_site`. It uses each boundary curtain cell at the release time minus its stored fractional `mean_age_particles_<d>`, requires those ages through OpenGHG's short-lifetime footprint schema, retrieves boundary history back to the earliest lagged time, and records the method in site attributes, acquisition facts and prepared provenance. The ModelScenario route rejects it. The lag helpers are vendored from openghg/openghg#1806 until a pinned OpenGHG release provides them.
