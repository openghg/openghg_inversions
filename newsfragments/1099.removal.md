`CorrelatedLognormalPrior` is no longer public and is removed from `openghg_inversions.models`, along with its `to_dataset` and `from_dataset` methods. Construct `CorrelatedPrior(mean, covariance)` instead: its default lognormal marginal gives the same prior and model graph. `prepare_co2_o2_inputs`, `Co2O2PreparedInputs.retained_prior` and the CO2 and linked CO2/O2 model builders now use `CorrelatedPrior` only.
