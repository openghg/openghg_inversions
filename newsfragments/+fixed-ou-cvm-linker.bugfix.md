Fixed-OU (and other NumPy-callback) models are now sampled with PyTensor's C/VM linker. Under the default Numba linker their likelihood ran through an object-mode callback that serialized the prepared arrays into every compiled function: a synthetic 7-site, 744-hour fixed-OU run peaked at 11.9 GiB instead of 1.0 GiB and took 2.4 times as long, and larger targets failed to compile once the serialized Op exceeded 2 GiB. `RhimeSampler` passes the C/VM mode to PyMC sampling, log-likelihood and predictive draws (log likelihood is now computed after sampling for these models), and the staged prior-predictive helpers do the same. Explicit `sample_kwargs["compile_kwargs"]` still take precedence.
