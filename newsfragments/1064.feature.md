Add versioned NetCDF/Zarr persistence for labelled native-covariance products and coherent Gaussian reductions in `openghg_inversions.covariance_artifacts`. Artifacts embed the covariance configuration and keep typed and MultiIndex labels, units, restriction provenance and a sparse restriction `Pi` without densifying it. They store every product as float64, except that the dense prolongation `U_*` may use float32. Each artifact records a `source_id` shared by dense, diagonal and coherent artifacts from the same inputs, and a `content_id` that loading verifies. `U_*` can also be omitted and rebuilt with `with_prolongation`. `CoherentGaussianReduction` now carries its `restriction`, a new required field for code that constructs it directly, and the derived bucket restriction records `restriction_source="derived"`.
