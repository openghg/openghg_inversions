Linked CO2/O2 configuration, handoffs and outputs accept CO2 in ppm with O2 as per-meg δ(O2/N2) when the prepared artifact declares that observable. The declaration (`openghg_inversions.inversion_data.o2n2.DeltaO2N2Declaration`, stored in preparation provenance under `o2_observable`) records the reference O2/N2 ratio and its source; configure it with `channels.o2.observable = "delta_o2_n2"`. The new `validate_co2_o2_channel_units` checks each channel against its observable, and undeclared per-meg labels are still rejected because unit parsers read `"per meg"` as 1e-6 mol/mol. Basic products label each channel's `units` and `observable`, and the δ channel also records its reference ratio. PARIS products keep CO2 concentrations and any requested fluxes, but skip the δ(O2/N2) concentration product, because the v04 template holds dry-air mole fractions.
