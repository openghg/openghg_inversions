The outer-flux option has a `"marginalise"` mode (`[model.outer_flux] mode = "marginalise"`). It holds the selected outer states at their prior mean and adds their prior uncertainty, `H_O Sigma_OO H_O^T`, to the fixed aggregation-error covariance as a low-rank term, so the outer flux is integrated out rather than held with zero uncertainty. Outer states correlated with the other states are replaced by their best linear predictor from them (the other states' design gains `H_O B`, and the error uses the conditional covariance). It is exact for Gaussian priors and a moment-level approximation under the LogNormal-copula prior; with `fixed_sources = ["ocean"]` it marginalises the outer ocean.
