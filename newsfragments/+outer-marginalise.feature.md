The outer-flux option has a `"marginalise"` mode (`[model.outer_flux] mode = "marginalise"`). It holds the selected outer states at their prior mean and adds their prior uncertainty, `H_O Sigma_OO H_O^T`, to the fixed aggregation-error covariance as a low-rank term, so the outer flux is integrated out rather than held with zero uncertainty. It is exact for linear Gaussian models and a moment-matched approximation under the LogNormal-copula prior; with `fixed_sources = ["ocean"]` it marginalises the outer ocean.
