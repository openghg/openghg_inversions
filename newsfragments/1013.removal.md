CO2 and linked CO2/O2 Python runners take one `boundary` choice (`MultiplicativeChoice` or `AdditiveChoice`, keyed by channel for linked recipes) instead of `use_bc` and the raw `bc_*` options, and no longer accept an already bound boundary. Centering weights come only from the prepared inputs; the runtime `bc_centering_weights` override is removed. Linked runners include only the channels given a choice, warn when prepared boundary transport is left without one, and accept `None` to disable a channel explicitly; configured runs still default every prepared channel boundary. Boundary metadata now records the requested `bc_frequency` and `bc_anchor_time` for every boundary, and linked metadata drops `additive_boundary_channels` and `boundary_prior` in favour of per-channel `boundary_metadata`.
