Surface acquisition no longer selects the boundary representation: `prepare_surface_site`, `acquire_surface_data` and `MergedData.from_options` always keep centered boundary operands and drop the `boundary_representation` argument. Their reference and tolerance arguments are now required with boundaries and are named `boundary_reference`, `boundary_reference_units` and `boundary_mass_tolerance` throughout. Preparation selects the representation with `select_boundary_representation`; ordinary results use the jointly normalized boundary mass.
