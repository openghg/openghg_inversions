Float32 runs can now draw from dense-covariance multivariate normals under the Numba linker, as in prior and posterior predictive sampling, `fixed_error` aggregation covariance and the CO2/O2 builders (#770). OpenGHG Inversions now requires PyTensor 3.3.5 or newer, which contains the upstream fix, and therefore PyMC 6.3.2 or newer.
