Staged CO2 outputs report complete native country totals. `prepare_co2_inputs(..., aggregation_error_rank=None)` persists a reduction's country `functional_products` in the prepared artifact after checking that their `reduction_identity` is the artifact's own (`Co2PreparedInputs.reduction_identity`), and checks it again on load (CO2 prepared-input schema 3 to 4; older artifacts must be prepared again). `co2_flux_country_functionals` builds `Q` from the signed reference flux before the affine map exists. Replay rebuilds each draw's residual and the fit's exact `(A + R)^-1` (additive-sigma, site-sigma and fixed-OU likelihoods), so country outputs, including sector totals, are labelled `uncertainty_scope="complete_native"` and record the `A` and noise model used. Artifacts without products keep `retained_state_conditional`.
