Retrieve the final release's flux in surface acquisition. OpenGHG 0.17 subtracts one second from a retrieval end date twice before slicing inclusively ([openghg#1817](https://github.com/openghg/openghg/issues/1817)), so the one-second pad dropped the last hourly flux row and integrated-only acquisition failed its coverage check on real stores. The flux query is now padded by two seconds.
