Registered statistics accept `register_stat(whole_dataset=True)` to receive every variable at once, and `register_stat(pointwise=True)` to declare per-point independence so `calculate_stats` keeps its bounded non-sample blocks. Custom statistics registered without options are still called once per variable on the complete dataset, as before.
`summarize_derived` adds shared arithmetic for sampled quantities such as baselines including offsets. Arithmetic runs in bounded blocks only when every requested statistic declares `pointwise=True`; otherwise it uses the complete dataset. It preserves operand and companion coordinate labels and rejects conflicting dataset metadata. Source reuse is within compatible blocks; lower-dimensional inputs may be reread across blocks or groups. Resident inputs, completed outputs and unbounded producers remain outside the workspace bound.
