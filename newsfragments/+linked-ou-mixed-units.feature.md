The linked CO2/O2 fixed-OU likelihood accepts different channel units, such as CO2 in ppm with per-meg δ(O2/N2) O2. Each `species:site` group has one unit, so a nonzero fixed amplitude must be a `co2:SITE`/`o2:SITE` mapping in each channel's units. Inferred amplitudes, which share one prior, still require a common unit. OU traces label amplitude units per group with an `ou_units` coordinate.
