The matched cached fixed-OU CO₂ and CO₂/O₂ models accept independent positive
amplitude priors through `site_amplitude_prior`, replacing the HalfNormal-only
`site_amplitude_prior_scale`. Conditional sampling uses the scientific model's
prior and transform, including bounded positive priors.
An explicit LogNormal `reparameterise=true` request warns and uses the direct
LogNormal variable required by the matched update, preserving the physical prior.
