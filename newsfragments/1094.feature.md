A basis built with fixed outer regions (`fix_outer_regions` for configured CO2, `fix_basis_outer_regions` for standard RHIME) now labels its states with `basis_group` (`"outer"` or `"inner"`), `basis_partition` and `region_in_partition`. The labels reach the prepared `alpha_prior_mean`, `alpha_prior_covariance` and `H`, and are saved with prepared inputs, so state selection by group (`StateActivity.fixed_groups`, the outer-flux option) works on configured preparations. Standard RHIME outputs from such a basis gain these state coordinates. Affine flux maps whose bucket basis carries state labels now reload.
