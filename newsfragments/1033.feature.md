Centered boundary transport now has static Dataset contracts: `RawCenteredTransport` for prepared or loaded operands, and `BoundCenteredTransport` (extended by `BoundAdditiveTransport` for weighted additive anomalies) for transport bound to one fit by `BoundCenteredTransport.bind`. Values remain ordinary `xarray.Dataset` objects; inspectable `xarray_validate` schemas check structure without computing lazy payloads. Explicit reference-shift and weight-normalization operations own their numerical checks after joint materialization. Contracts are declared with the new `openghg_inversions.xr_contracts.xr_contract` decorator.
