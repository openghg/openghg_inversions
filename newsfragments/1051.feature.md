Prepare linked CO2/O2 inputs from native per-source responses with `prepare_multisource_native_co2_o2_inputs` (in `openghg_inversions.rhime.co2.co2_o2_native_preparation`). It reuses the configured CO2 route's per-source native priors, including `marginal` and `latent_correlation_floor`, and takes an explicit map of each native source to its retained `(source, tracer_scope)`, so GPP/TER/FF are shared while the CO2 and O2 ocean sources stay independent with their own priors. One coherent reduction over both tracers' observations gives the retained prior, both effective sensitivities and the joint unresolved covariance, including the CO2/O2 cross block. Configured acquisition of the two channels follows separately.
