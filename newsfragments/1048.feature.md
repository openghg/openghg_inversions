Added `openghg_inversions.native_b_calibration`, which calibrates per-source (optionally per-class) native prior covariance amplitudes so that the prior SD of declared regional or country flux totals matches relative or absolute targets. It evaluates functional variances through the separable covariance action without forming dense `B`, takes the correlation length explicitly, stabilizes relative targets of cancelling totals, offers geometric-mean or median statistics, calibrates each month (or other slice) independently, and reports each target as met, unmet or at a bound. Frozen amplitude policies are evaluated only with an explicit `allow_broadcast=True`, and per-slice `IndependentSourceCovariance` objects are built directly from the calibrated amplitudes.
