Added `openghg_inversions.native_b_calibration`, which calibrates per-source (optionally per-class) native prior covariance amplitudes so that the prior SD of declared regional or country flux totals matches relative or absolute targets. It evaluates functional variances through the separable covariance action without forming dense `B`, takes the correlation length explicitly, calibrates each month (or other slice) independently, reports achieved against target SDs, evaluates frozen amplitude policies, and builds the matching `IndependentSourceCovariance`.
