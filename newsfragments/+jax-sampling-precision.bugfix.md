NumPyro and BlackJAX sampling now runs at the model's precision. PyTensor's JAX backend fixes `jax_enable_x64` from `floatX` on first import, so `JAX_ENABLE_X64=1` was silently turned off under the float32 default, and float64 graphs built later (for example with `change_flags(floatX="float64")`) were truncated to float32. `JAX_ENABLE_X64=1` now selects `floatX=float64` unless `PYTENSOR_FLAGS` names `floatX`; JAX backends enable x64 for float64 models, warn when sampling a float32 model, and record the precision in the trace attribute `sampler_precision`.
