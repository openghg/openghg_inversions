Boundary components now bind scientific choices once and supply shared contributions to ordinary and cached CO2 models, including mean-only additive fits. Boundary-period coordinates in CLI outputs use interval-start timestamps instead of ordinal integers. CO2 and CO2/O2 boundary tables select their family with `type = "multiplicative"` (the default) or `type = "additive"`, and reject options that belong to another family; outputs record the family as `boundary_formulation`. Earlier internal staged manifests and prepared/posterior artifacts are unsupported; prepare and sample again.

Linked CO2/O2 basic products retain fitted boundary mean-shift and anomaly coefficients with their declared units and labelled coefficient dimensions. PARIS concentration products retain their existing names and meaning.
