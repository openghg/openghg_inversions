Additive boundary choices now state the structure of their anomalies: weighted centering (default), per-period curtain contrasts or independent state anomalies, with an optional mean shift per boundary period; the mean shift responds through the boundary mass sensitivity. A new `AnomalyScalingChoice` scales the prescribed boundary anomaly with dimensionless coefficients about a stored, explicit or whole-window reference. Both are bound to the prepared centered operands of each fit.
