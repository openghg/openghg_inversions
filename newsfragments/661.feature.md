Configure the new boundary correction structures through `[model.boundary]` keys (`anomalies`, `mean_by_period`, and `type = "anomaly_scaling"` with `scale_sd` and `reference`). Configure the outer-flux option with `[model.outer_flux]` (`mode = "fixed"`, `outer_groups`, `fixed_sources`), or a top-level `[outer_flux]` table for linked CO2/O2, in ordinary, cached and staged runs.
