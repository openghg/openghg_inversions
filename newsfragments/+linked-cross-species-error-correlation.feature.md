The linked CO2/O2 likelihoods (fixed independent error and fixed OU) accept `cross_species_error_correlation`, a fixed correlation between the independent errors of CO2 and O2 rows at the same site and time. It is either one value or a site-keyed mapping, and is also available as `likelihood.cross_species_error_correlation` in configuration. The cross covariance is ρ·σ_CO2·σ_O2, in the product of the channel units, so it works with ppm CO2 and per-meg δ(O2/N2). The shared Gaussian likelihoods gain a `paired_error_correlation` option (`openghg_inversions.models.paired_error`), which is exact for none, diagonal, dense and low-rank aggregation error and for fixed OU. Rows with a missing (NaT) time are never paired, and pairs with zero correlation add no factor column. Traces record each row's correlation and partner.
