The linked CO2/O2 likelihoods (fixed independent error, fixed OU through the cached fixed-OU sampler, and inferred OU) accept a fixed model error, `model_error_sd` (by channel or `species:SITE`, in each channel's units, or `channels.<name>.model_error_sd` in configuration), and `cross_species_error_correlation`, a fixed correlation ρ between the model errors of CO2 and O2 rows at the same site and time. ρ is one value or a site-keyed mapping, also available as `likelihood.cross_species_error_correlation`, and requires the model error: the covariance is A + D_obs + Σ_model(ρ) [+ OU], so measurement error, aggregation covariance and OU terms stay uncorrelated. The cross covariance is ρ·m_CO2·m_O2, in the product of the channel units, so it works with ppm CO2 and per-meg δ(O2/N2). The shared Gaussian likelihoods gain a `paired_error_correlation` option on `fixed_model_mismatch` (`openghg_inversions.models.paired_error`), exact for none, dense and low-rank aggregation error and for fixed OU; correlated pairs are exact 2×2 blocks, so independent and low-rank likelihoods stay linear in the pairs, and pairs with zero correlation keep the original numerical path. `linked_pair_correlation_diagnostic` reports the CO2–O2 correlation the aggregation covariance alone implies and the total correlation, and runs with ρ record a by-site summary in their metadata. Rows with a missing site or time are never paired, correlations that round to ±1 at the working precision are rejected, and traces record the model error and each row's correlation and partner.
