Configured CO2 can select an exact fixed-noise Gaussian estimator with `[sampling] method = "gaussian"`. It solves the posterior of the linear-Gaussian CO2 graph (Gaussian retained prior with the prepared arithmetic moments, Normal boundary and offset priors, fixed `mf_error² + fixed_model_mismatch²` plus aggregation covariance) by Paige's generalised least squares, without inverting or whitening the noise covariance, and writes independent exact draws in the NUTS trace layout plus a `gaussian_posterior` group with the exact moments. NUTS remains the default.
