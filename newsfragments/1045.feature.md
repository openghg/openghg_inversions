**Scientific policy change:** generated RHIME bases are now fitted to response-magnitude weights from the filtered observations' existing `fp_x_flux` responses, replacing mean footprint × mean flux of the first requested source. Standard and nested RHIME use `mean_o(abs(fp_x_flux))`, so sectors sharing one state cancel before the absolute value; multisector RHIME uses `sum_s mean_o(abs(R_s))` from `fp_x_flux_sectoral`. Absolute values are taken per observation, and observations are pooled across sites by count. Generated partitions, and fitted results, may change; reusing a saved operator is unaffected. Generation no longer needs raw `fp` or history-resolved `fp_time_resolved`, so merged inputs may omit them unless another selected consumer, such as the `local_influence` filter, needs them.
