Add `SuppliedRestriction`, a supplied-`Pi`-first retained projection strategy for `project_native_covariance` and `reduce_native_gaussian`. It accepts a caller-defined physical restriction, such as the source-blocked absolute-flux-weighted regional mean used by verification-games production runs, and derives `C_alpha`, the covariance-natural lift `U_*`, and `H U_*` from it. It refuses a signed restriction for LogNormal retained states and keeps sparse restrictions and basis prolongations sparse.
