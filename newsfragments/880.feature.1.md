Ordinary CO2 configured execution and saved phases share scientific operations,
with reusable coherent preparation, manifest-only sampling and graph-free replay
of concentration and conditional affine flux products. Requested dates survive
filtering and imports; inference validates retained-site mappings.

explicit, and imported prepared/affine files are referenced in place.

alias. Python `run(model="co2")` selects the same configured `run_co2` entry
point and returns its scientific output view.
Configured execution starts from prepared inputs; native acquisition and
coherent-reduction construction remain separate work.
