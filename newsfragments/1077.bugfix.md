Saved DataTree artifacts and traces no longer decode numeric variables with only a time `units` attribute, such as a fixed-OU `ou_tau_hours` in hours, into timedeltas. Only variables xarray wrote as timedeltas (`dtype` attribute) are decoded, matching xarray's announced future default. Previously a 4-hour time scale reloaded as a timedelta whose nanosecond count could be read as 1.44e13 hours.
