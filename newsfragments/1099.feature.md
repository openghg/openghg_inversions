Linked CO2/O2 prepared inputs can carry a per-state prior marginal (`"lognormal"`, `"normal"`, `"weibull"` or `"gamma"`) and the opt-in `latent_correlation_floor`: pass `prior_marginal` and `latent_correlation_floor` to `prepare_co2_o2_inputs`. Both are saved with the preparation, and the ordinary and cached linked runners build the copula prior from them and record its marginals and any latent-correlation repair in the model metadata. Preparations without a marginal keep the lognormal prior unchanged. The linked prepared-input schema version is now 6, so prepare saved linked inputs again.
