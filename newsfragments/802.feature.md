Live runners now accept `sampler="default"` (also the omitted-argument default)
or a configured `RhimeSampler`. Replace explicit `sampler=None` with `"default"`
or omit the argument. Sampler option containers are isolated per invocation;
`idata_kwargs` cannot override model-owned `coords` or `dims`, checked once
at sampler construction.

Results without a live sampler now use `sampler=None`. Staged postprocessing
retains saved sampler provenance as metadata without running inference. Missing
or unparseable descriptions do not block products or acquire current defaults.

Group RHIME acquisition/preparation choices in `RhimeConfig.data: DataConfig` and add reusable standard preparation/sample manifests. Compatible model choices can share one preparation, and saved samples can produce new outputs without rebuilding a model or repeating inference. Standard staged CLI commands now take one manifest; historical standard staged envelopes and multi-artifact argument lists must be regenerated. Existing numerical codecs and final product formats are preserved.

Preserve exact basis exports outside checkpoint directories and reject explicitly selected traces missing scientific output state such as `x` before completing a saved handoff. Full and phase-only frontends translate aliases after raw overrides, with phase errors reporting all unsuitable keys.
