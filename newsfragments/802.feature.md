Live runners now accept `sampler="default"` (also the omitted-argument default)
or a configured `RhimeSampler`. Replace explicit `sampler=None` with `"default"`
or omit the argument. Sampler option containers are isolated per invocation;
`idata_kwargs` cannot override model-owned `coords` or `dims`, checked once
at sampler construction.

Results without a live sampler now use `sampler=None`. Staged postprocessing
retains saved sampler provenance as metadata without running inference. Missing
or unparseable descriptions do not block products or acquire current defaults.

Group RHIME preparation choices in `PreparationConfig` and add reusable standard preparation/sample manifests. Compatible model choices can share one preparation, and saved samples can produce new outputs without rebuilding a model or repeating inference. Standard staged CLI commands now take one manifest; historical standard staged envelopes and multi-artifact argument lists must be regenerated. Existing numerical codecs and final product formats are preserved.
