CO2 configurations can now build a new preparation from `[prepared_inputs.acquisition]`, `basis`, `observations` and `native_prior` tables instead of importing `prepared_inputs.path`; `run_co2` and `rhime prepare --model co2` acquire surface data, reduce the native prior coherently and save the prepared inputs with their affine companion. The configured `[model.boundary]` choice selects the prepared boundary representation (centered for `additive`, ordinary by default for `multiplicative`), and `prepared_inputs.boundary_representation` can request `"centered"` explicitly. Every stage now rejects a preparation that the configured boundary choice cannot use.
