CO2 configurations can now build a new preparation from `[prepared_inputs.acquisition]`, `basis`, `observations` and `native_prior` tables instead of importing `prepared_inputs.path`; `run_co2` and `rhime prepare --model co2` acquire surface data, reduce the native prior coherently and save the prepared inputs with their affine companion. The configured `[model.boundary]` choice selects the prepared boundary representation (centered for `additive` and `anomaly_scaling`, ordinary by default for `multiplicative`), and `prepared_inputs.boundary_representation` can request `"centered"` explicitly. Every stage now rejects a preparation that the configured boundary choice cannot use. Acquisition options (site selectors, transport modes and boundary reference and tolerance) are checked when the configuration is read, before any retrieval. Generated bases support `weighted` and `quadtree`; `region_constrained` waits on configured class maps (#454).
